Unfold: Private Journal

Android · Health & Fitness · PEGI 3 · Free with in-app purchases

A journal that notices what keeps coming back in your thoughts. Unfold is a calm place to put a thought down without being judged, then to understand it. No feed, no streaks, no scores. Just your words, kept safe, and a clearer view of what they say over time.

Get it on Google Play

Write it down, as it comes

  • Write Open the app and start. A question, never an instruction: “What's on your mind right now?”
  • Moods as colours Finish each entry with how you feel. Moods are colours, not emoji or ratings.

See what your days look like

  • Today Your space for the day, with a memory from a month ago, in your own words.
  • Journal All your entries, easy to reread and find again.
  • Calendar Your month at a glance, coloured by your moods.
  • My emotions How your feelings change from week to week, and what keeps showing up on your best days.

Understand what keeps coming back (subscription)

  • Reflected entries When you finish an entry, Unfold reads it back and highlights the emotions in your words.
  • Patterns Unfold notices the words, moments and themes that recur — “Sunday evenings weigh on you the most.” Each pattern shows the entries it comes from, so you can check for yourself.
  • Ask my journal Ask a question in your own words, like “When was the last time I felt this calm?” The answer comes from your own entries, with sources.
  • Summary A short overview of a period, with its themes and key entries, ready to bring to a therapy session or an important conversation.

Unfold observes. It doesn't advise, diagnose or lecture. It tells you what happened and why it thinks so, then leaves the rest to you.

Private by design

  • No email, no sign-up form Your journal starts with an anonymous account.
  • Encrypted entries Your entries are encrypted and remain yours. Data is encrypted in transit and never shared with third parties.
  • No ads, ever Your writing is never sold.
  • Delete everything, anytime Erase all your data at any time from the “Me” tab. Read Unfold's privacy policy for details.

Free and Premium

Writing, moods, your journal, the calendar and your memories are free, with no limit on entries. A subscription unlocks the advanced features: reflected entries, patterns, Ask my journal and summaries. You can try it before committing, then manage or cancel it at any time from Google Play.

Unfold works offline and syncs in the background.

Designed for calm

Soft colours, plenty of space, nothing competing with your thoughts. Unfold is built on one idea: less interface, more reflection.
