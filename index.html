What we do

  • Product design From the first idea to polished interfaces that follow Apple and Google platform guidelines.
  • Native-quality mobile development iOS and Android apps built with React Native, TypeScript and native Swift / Kotlin modules where needed.
  • Publishing & maintenance We handle App Store and Google Play releases and keep our apps updated for every new OS version.
  • Customer support Every user can reach us directly — see our support page.

Our commitments

  • Privacy first We collect as little data as possible, never sell personal data and explain everything in our privacy policy.
  • Real people behind the apps Questions and bug reports are answered by the person who builds the app.
  • Long-term care We maintain our apps over time, fix issues quickly and listen to user feedback.
